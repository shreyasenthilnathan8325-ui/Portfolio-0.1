Background

Write two or three paragraphs here about where you grew up, what you studied at school, and the moment the subject you are in now started to interest you. Concrete beats general: a teacher, a book, a project that went badly and taught you something.

Keep it honest rather than impressive. Admissions readers see hundreds of these, and the specific ones are the ones they remember.

The decision to go abroad

Explain what you are looking for that you cannot get at home: a lab, a supervisor, a method, a particular dataset, a city. Name it. A vague wish to "experience another culture" reads very differently from wanting to work with a specific group on a specific problem.
